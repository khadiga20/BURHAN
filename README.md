# BURHAN | بُرهان

### Understand the WHY Behind Mathematics & Science

**BURHAN** is a bilingual educational web platform designed to help students move beyond memorization and develop a deeper understanding of **Mathematics and Science** through reasoning, observation, and proof.

> **Don't just memorize the answer. Understand why.**

---

## ✨ Overview

BURHAN presents Mathematics and Science as structured systems of understanding rather than collections of formulas and facts.

The platform provides dedicated learning experiences for:

* 📐 **Mathematics**
* 🔬 **Science**
* 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Profiles & Mentorship**
* 💬 **Direct Teacher Contact**
* 📄 **Teacher CVs**
* 🌐 **Arabic / English Interface**
* 🌙 **Dark / Light Mode**

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

The educational philosophy behind BURHAN is simple:

**Question → Reasoning → Understanding**

The platform is built around the idea that true learning begins when students stop asking only **"How?"** and start asking **"Why?"**

## 🚀 Features

### 🌐 Bilingual Experience

Full Arabic and English support with automatic RTL/LTR direction handling.

### 🌙 Dark & Light Themes

A modern theme system with persistent user preferences.

### 📚 Dedicated Learning Worlds

Separate experiences for Mathematics and Science, each with its own visual identity and educational content.

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher Profiles

Detailed educator profiles highlighting:

* Professional experience
* Academic qualifications
* Areas of expertise
* Teaching philosophy
* Contact information

### 📄 CV Integration

Teacher CV documents can be accessed directly from their profiles.

### 💬 Direct Contact

Students can contact teachers through:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* Phone

### 🎬 Cinematic Introduction

A short branded introduction establishes BURHAN's core educational philosophy before entering the platform.

### 📱 Responsive Design

Designed to provide a consistent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

### ♿ Accessibility

Includes accessibility-oriented features such as semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly navigation, and skip-to-content support.

## 💡 Design Philosophy

BURHAN follows an editorial and academic visual direction rather than a traditional educational-platform aesthetic.

The interface combines:

* Mathematical notation
* Scientific visual language
* Structured typography
* Minimal editorial layouts
* Indigo and amber subject identities
* Motion-based storytelling

The goal is to make the interface itself communicate the brand's philosophy of **precision, reasoning, and understanding**.
