The BURHAN Methodology

A proof assembled step by step.

We reject formula recitation. Every topic is presented through three structured stages of deduction.

01 | QUESTION The Inquiry

The Problem Begins with Asking Why

We start not with a formula to memorize, but with a fundamental question about how mathematical structures behave and why a specific pattern exists.

Initial Problem State: x² + 5x + 6 = 0 → Why does factoring work?
02 | REASONING Logical Deduction

Assembling the Logical Sequence

Step by step, we break down geometric relationships, algebraic transformations, and axiomatic deductions until every line logically necessitates the next.

Deductive Sequence: (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0 ∴ x = -2 or x = -3
03 | UNDERSTANDING Self-Evident Truth

The Truth Rendered Self-Evident

The solution is no longer a trick remembered for an exam; it is a permanent mental model of how mathematical reality operates.

Conclusion: Q.E.D. — Quod Erat Demonstrandum | وهو المطلوب إثباته
