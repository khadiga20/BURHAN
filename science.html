The BURHAN Methodology

A natural law discovered step by step.

We reject formula recitation. Every scientific phenomenon is presented through three structured stages of empirical discovery.

01 | OBSERVATION The Phenomenon

The Discovery Begins with Asking Why

We start not with an equation to memorize, but with observing a physical event, chemical reaction, or biological system to identify underlying patterns.

Observed Phenomenon: Object accelerates under force → Why F = ma?
02 | HYPOTHESIS Controlled Testing

Testing Variables & Formulating Models

Step by step, we isolate physical forces, chemical reactants, and cellular processes until cause and effect necessitate the mathematical model.

Experimental Process: F = ma  |  PV = nRT  |  E = mc²
03 | NATURAL LAW Universal Principle

The Law Rendered Self-Evident

The formula is no longer a trick remembered for an exam; it is a permanent mental model of how the physical universe operates.

Conclusion: Natural Principle Verified | القانون الطبيعي المؤكد
